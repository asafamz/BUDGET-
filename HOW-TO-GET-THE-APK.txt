ASAFAMZ BUDGET - PUT IT ONLINE AND GET AN ANDROID APK
=====================================================
Total time: about 20 minutes. Everything here is free.

PART 1 - PUT THE APP ONLINE (GitHub Pages)
1. On github.com create a new PUBLIC repository, e.g. "budget".
2. Upload EVERYTHING in this folder, keeping the folders as they are:
   index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js, .nojekyll, icons/, screenshots/
   (.nojekyll is hidden on some computers; it must be uploaded. It lets
   GitHub serve the .well-known folder you add in Part 3.)
3. Repository > Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch",
   Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save.
4. After a minute your app is live at:
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/budget/
   Open that link in Chrome on your phone. It works like the app already.
   Chrome may offer "Install app"; that alone puts it on your home screen.

PART 2 - TURN IT INTO AN APK (PWABuilder, made by Microsoft)
1. Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com and paste your link from Part 1.
2. Click "Package for stores" > Android > "Generate Package".
   In Options set:
     Package ID:  com.asafamz.budget
     App name:    Asafamz Budget      Launcher name: Asafamz
     Signing key: Create new
3. Download the zip. Inside you get:
     - an .apk file  -> install this on your phone
     - an .aab file  -> this is what you upload to the Google Play Store later
     - assetlinks.json -> needed in Part 3
     - signing.keystore + signing-key-info.txt -> BACK THESE UP SAFELY.
       You need the same key for every future update on the Play Store.
       If you lose it you cannot update the app.

PART 3 - REMOVE THE BROWSER BAR (recommended)
Without this step the APK works but shows a small address bar at the top.
1. In your GitHub repo create a folder named  .well-known
2. Upload the assetlinks.json from PWABuilder into it, so it opens at:
      https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/.well-known/assetlinks.json
   IMPORTANT: this must be at the ROOT of your github.io address, which
   means it has to be in a repo named exactly  YOUR-USERNAME.github.io
   If your app is in a repo called "budget", create that second repo just
   for the .well-known folder (with a .nojekyll file), OR host the app
   itself in the YOUR-USERNAME.github.io repo. A custom domain avoids this.

PART 4 - INSTALL THE APK ON YOUR PHONE
1. Send the .apk to your phone (WhatsApp to yourself, Google Drive, USB).
2. Tap it. Android asks to allow "Install unknown apps" for that app
   (Files, Chrome or WhatsApp). Allow it, go back, tap Install.
3. Google Play Protect may warn because it didn't come from the Play Store.
   That's normal for your own test builds; choose "Install anyway".

MOVING YOUR EXISTING DATA
Each web address keeps its own data. If you have entries in another copy
of the app (the claude.ai link or a downloaded file), open that copy >
Settings > Back up data > Copy or Download, then in the new app >
Settings > Restore from backup. The APK shares data with Chrome for the
same address, so entries made in Chrome on your github.io link appear
in the app too.

UPDATING THE APP LATER
Upload the new index.html to GitHub, and change the VERSION line at the
top of sw.js (e.g. asafamz-2.1.0 -> asafamz-2.1.1). The installed APK
picks up the new version the next time it's opened with internet.
You don't need a new APK unless you change the name, icon or package.

GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP
Once the app is online you can switch on Google Drive backup. Follow
SETUP-GOOGLE-DRIVE-for-seller.txt and use your github.io address (or your
own domain) as the "Authorized JavaScript origin".
